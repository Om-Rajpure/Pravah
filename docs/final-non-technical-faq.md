# PRAVAAH — Final Non-Technical FAQ (General Audience)

### 1. What is PRAVAAH in simple terms?
PRAVAAH (प्रवाह - *flow*) is an intelligent city platform designed to manage crowd movement during massive events like Ganesh Chaturthi in Mumbai. It acts as an early-warning system and intelligent guide that prevents dangerous crowd crushes before they happen.

### 2. What problem does it solve?
During Ganesh Chaturthi, millions of people rush to the same pandals and railway stations at the same time. The problem is not that Mumbai lacks space — it is that crowd pressure is unevenly distributed. PRAVAAH spots upcoming bottlenecks hours in advance and helps balance the flow across the city.

### 3. How does it help citizens and travelers?
Citizens can open the mobile-friendly PRAVAAH Visitor Guide to check live crowd levels at their destination. If an area like Gateway of India is packed, PRAVAAH recommends a nearby, less-crowded alternative (like Marine Drive) and gives clear, plain-language advice on the best time to visit.

### 4. Does PRAVAAH track my phone or personal location?
**No.** PRAVAAH does not collect your name, phone number, email, or exact GPS coordinates. It only looks at neighborhood-level crowd density (like turnstile counts). Location sharing is completely optional, approximate, and never saved.

### 5. What makes PRAVAAH different from other dashboards?
Most systems only tell operators what is already happening (e.g. "Curry Road is congested"). PRAVAAH predicts what will happen in 2–3 hours, tests solutions on a computer simulation first, and recommends the exact action needed to prevent the bottleneck.

### 6. Can PRAVAAH work for other festivals or cities?
**Yes.** While calibrated to Mumbai during Ganesh Chaturthi, PRAVAAH's network model and prediction engine can be configured for any mega-event, such as the Kumbh Mela, major sporting events, or transit hubs in other global metropolitan areas.
