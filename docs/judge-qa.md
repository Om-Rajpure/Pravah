# PRAVAAH — Technical Defense & Judge FAQ

Comprehensive technical and design answers for Hackathon judges and technical reviewers.

---

## 1. Core Architecture & AI

### Q1: Why not just use an LLM (Large Language Model) with an agent prompt?
**Answer**:
LLMs are probabilistic text generators; they cannot perform deterministic network flow simulations, guarantee physical capacity constraints, or evaluate exact mathematical counterfactuals.
- An LLM cannot deterministically simulate 10,000 agents traversing a 76-edge transit graph at 5-minute discrete time steps.
- An LLM can hallucinate numbers (e.g. "pressure drops by 40%"), whereas PRAVAAH runs a **true discrete-time agent simulation** where `target_before - target_after == 18` is a verified mathematical consequence of flow redirection.
- PRAVAAH uses a **LightGBM residual prediction model** (`pravaah-prediction-v1`, MAE: 1.026) layered over a physics-based graph propagation baseline.
- If language generation is needed in the future for notifications, it should be grounded in PRAVAAH's verified state, not generated freely.

### Q2: How does the Prediction Engine work under the hood?
**Answer**:
Our prediction engine operates in three stages:
1. **Physics & Ingress Baseline**: Calculates arrival velocity vs. platform clearance rates per zone based on network connectivity and historical time-of-day arrival curves.
2. **Network Flow Propagation**: Uses Dijkstra routing over our 30-node / 76-edge NetworkX graph to compute upstream/downstream pressure transfer times.
3. **LightGBM Residual Correction**: A gradient-boosted decision tree model trained on synthetic historical telemetry (features: `current_pressure`, `platform_saturation_ratio`, `adjacent_edge_loads`, `hour_of_day`, `is_peak_window`) that predicts non-linear residuals.
4. **Horizons**: Outputs calibrated forecasts across 4 standard planning windows: +30m, +60m, +120m, +180m.

### Q3: How do you evaluate and rank intervention candidates?
**Answer**:
The Intervention Engine evaluates 25 distinct combinations of relief destinations (Thane, Vashi, Bandra, Andheri, Dadar) across 6 dosage levels (5%, 10%, 15%, 18%, 20%, 25%).
- **Constraint Filtering**: Discards candidates where transit corridor edges are disrupted/closed or where the relief zone has `<15%` spare capacity.
- **Counterfactual Run**: Executes an isolated simulation step for each surviving candidate.
- **Multi-Objective Loss Function**:
  $$\text{Score} = w_1 \cdot \Delta P_{\text{target}} - w_2 \cdot \Delta P_{\text{spillover}} - w_3 \cdot \text{Cost}(\text{travel\_time}) - w_4 \cdot \text{CorridorRisk}$$
  Where $w_1=0.45, w_2=0.25, w_3=0.15, w_4=0.15$. The candidate with the highest net score is selected.

## 2. Privacy, Data Governance & Ethics

### Q4: How can you guide crowds without tracking individual GPS locations?
**Answer**:
PRAVAAH follows a strict **Data Minimization and Aggregation** architecture:
- **Zone-Level Telemetry**: We only observe aggregate density (occupancy / capacity) at the neighborhood level, similar to turnstile counts or cellular tower sector loads.
- **K-Anonymity (K=10)**: Any group or bucket with fewer than 10 visitors is suppressed from public and operator outputs (`visible: false`).
- **Zero PII**: The visitor API never requires login, cookies, device identifiers, or GPS coordinates.
- **Public-Safe Serialization**: Internal floats (`73.82`) are quantized to visitor-friendly labels (`HIGH`), and internal network edge IDs are converted to operational status (`OPEN / SLOW / DISRUPTED`).

### Q5: Is the data real or simulated?
**Answer**:
In this prototype, data is generated by our deterministic simulation engine calibrated to real Mumbai geography during Ganesh Chaturthi 2026:
- Real geographic coordinates for all 11 monitored zones, 12 railway stations, and 4 major pandal epicenters.
- Real road network topology (Dr. BA Road, Eastern Freeway, Tilak Bridge) and suburban railway lines (Central, Western, Harbour).
- Realistic arrival curves (10,000 synthetic visitors, peak 18:00–22:00, calibrated based on Mumbai municipal crowd historical patterns).
- Explicit disclosure: Every screen and API response displays the `SIMULATED · SYNTHETIC` data label.

## 3. Scale, Reliability & Systems

### Q6: How does the system scale to millions of visitors?
**Answer**:
1. **Separation of Concerns**:
   - **Micro-Simulation**: Agent-level simulation is used for tactical corridor modeling.
   - **Macroscopic Flow Aggregation**: For citywide operations, agents are aggregated into flow rate matrices ($Q_{ij}$ in persons/hour) across graph edges.
2. **High-Performance In-Memory DuckDB**:
   - Tabular state queries run in `<5ms`. Zero file locking.
3. **Sub-second Inference**:
   - Multi-horizon LightGBM predictions evaluate in `<45ms`.
   - 25-candidate intervention simulations complete in `<80ms`.
4. **Caching & Versioning**:
   - Predictions and interventions are cached by `(network_version, scenario_version, sim_step)` and only recomputed when underlying state changes.

### Q7: What happens when network disruptions or bad weather occur?
**Answer**:
PRAVAAH includes an **overlay-based Scenario Injector**:
- When the Central Line Disruption is activated, the network graph immediately closes affected edges (`Parel ↔ Curry Road`).
- The system automatically triggers a 5-stage causal cascade: `TRIGGER → NETWORK → FLOW → PRESSURE → RESPONSE`.
- Previous recommendations are immediately flagged as **STALE**, and the engine re-runs candidate generation over the surviving graph topology.

---

### Q8: What if an external tile service or API fails during live operations?
**Answer**:
- **Layered Graceful Degradation**: If MapLibre tile servers are unreachable, the application falls back gracefully — all telemetry, prediction tables, Glass Box explainability panels, and visitor guidance continue operating without disruption.
- **Frontend Error Boundaries**: Every major page and component is wrapped in React Error Boundaries to prevent cascading application crashes.
- **Centralized Health and Readiness Probes**: `/api/health` and `/api/ready` provide instant diagnosis of service status.
