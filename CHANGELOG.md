# Changelog — PRAVAAH

All notable changes to the PRAVAAH platform are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-25 (Hackathon Final Release)

### Added
- **Phase 1-3**: Real Mumbai geographic foundation with MapLibre GL JS, OpenFreeMap Positron/Liberty tiles, and 11 monitored zone coordinates.
- **Phase 5**: 10,000-agent deterministic crowd simulation engine (`DEMO_SEED=20260908`).
- **Phase 6**: Multimodal Mumbai transit network model (30 nodes, 76 directed edges, Dijkstra routing).
- **Phase 7**: Network-aware prediction engine with LightGBM residual forecasting across 4 horizons (+30m, +60m, +120m, +180m).
- **Phase 8**: Counterfactual intervention engine evaluating 25 candidate dosages with simulated impact.
- **Phase 9**: What-if scenario injector with 5-stage causal cascades (Central Line Rail Disruption, Heavy Rain, Road Closure).
- **Phase 10**: Glass Box explainability with causal trace stages, plain-language feature drivers, and immutable decision audit trails.
- **Phase 11**: Privacy governance, K-Anonymity suppression ($K=10$), and mobile-first visitor guidance UI.
- **Phase 12**: Persistent Demo Mode Bar, 6 canonical timeline events, and atomic `/api/demo/reset`.
- **Phase 13**: Production security hardening (CSP, X-Frame-Options, readiness probe `/api/ready`, standardized JSON error envelope, Docker/Nginx configuration).
- **Phase 14**: Interactive Judge Tour Modal (`JudgeTourModal.jsx`), 2-minute elevator pitch, 5-minute live demo script, and judge technical defense FAQ.
- **Phase 15 & 16**: Formal intelligence validation, 20-cycle determinism stress testing, 46-test QA matrix, and submission freeze.
